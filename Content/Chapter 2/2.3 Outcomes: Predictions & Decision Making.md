### 2.3 Outcomes: Predictions & Decision Making

Predictive Evaluation: Evaluating unseen data metrics against historical patterns to forecast future events or statuses.

Regression Metrics: The statistical forecasting of continuous numeric outputs, such as fluctuating stock values or temperatures.

Generative Outputs: Computational predictions forecasting the most likely next linguistic token in a sentence or next pixel configuration in an image.

Autonomous Execution: Immediate real-world adjustments driven by system outcomes without human intervention (e.g., a self-driving car braking).

Recommendation Engines: Automated decision platforms selecting optimal media choices to display next, maximizing active user engagement.
