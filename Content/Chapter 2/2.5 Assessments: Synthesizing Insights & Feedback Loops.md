### 2.5 Assessments: Synthesizing Insights & Feedback Loops
Metrics Evaluation: Analyzing cumulative accuracy, precision, and recall parameters to verify safe, predictable model behavior across environments.

Generalization Check: Verifying that a deployed model hasn't simply memorized training inputs, preventing the system error of overfitting.

Data Drift Prevention: Implementing live feedback loops to adjust model logic when real-world conditions naturally evolve away from training profiles.

Active Learning Loop: Flagging low-confidence operational cases to route to human experts for labeling, continuously restarting the 5-step cycle.
