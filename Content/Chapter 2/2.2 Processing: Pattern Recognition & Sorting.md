### 2.2 Processing: Pattern Recognition & Sorting
Algorithmic Scanning: Mathematical data analysis to isolate correlations, spatial clusters, and structural trends hidden from human sight.
Neural Networks: Layers of interconnected mathematical calculation nodes loosely inspired by biological brain architectures that extract abstract features.
Statistical Modeling: Algorithmic use of probability distributions to determine the likelihood of specific data points co-occurring.
Vector Embeddings: Transforming data items into a high-dimensional mathematical space for quantitative representation.
Data Clustering: Grouping similar data points together using unsupervised algorithms based on shared spatial attributes without human instruction.
Data Classification: Sorting incoming information into predefined categories using supervised algorithms (e.g., routing emails to spam or inbox).
