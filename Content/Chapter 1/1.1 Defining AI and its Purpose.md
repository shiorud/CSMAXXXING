### 1.1 Defining AI and its Purpose 

Core Definition: Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, specifically computer systems, to execute complex cognitive tasks.

Primary Objective: Augment human capability, automate repetitive or hazardous workflows, and isolate structural patterns in datasets too massive for manual analysis.

Common Misconception: AI implies conscious, emotional systems. Current technologies are purely mathematical pattern optimization tools with no self-awareness.
