### 6.1 What is Symbolic AI?

Symbolic AI represents knowledge using **symbols**: names for objects and concepts, plus relationships between them. It then reasons by applying logical **rules** to those symbols (DataCamp, 2023). Instead of learning from huge piles of data, a human *writes the knowledge down*.

A classic example is a medical rule of the form:

> IF the patient has a fever AND a cough AND difficulty breathing, THEN the patient may have pneumonia.

The system checks the facts about a patient against its rules and draws a conclusion. A collection of facts and rules like this is called a **knowledge base**, and a program built on one that gives advice in a specialist area is an **expert system**.
