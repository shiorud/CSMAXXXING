### 1.2 The Societal and Economic Impact of AI

Labor Evolution: Automation of routine cognitive and manual tasks displaces specific job roles while creating entirely new technical industries.

Productivity Gains: Supply chain optimization, predictive maintenance, and streamlined workflows lower corporate operational costs and drive macroeconomic GDP growth.

Hyper-Personalization: Real-time consumer behavior tracking allows businesses to tailor product suites, advertisements, and digital interfaces to individual profiles.

Healthcare Revolution: Automated medical imaging analysis enables early anomaly detection, while molecular simulations drastically accelerate drug discovery timelines.

The Digital Divide: High capital, compute, and data requirements risk a widening economic wealth gap between tech-dominant entities and developing regions.

Algorithmic Bias: Systems trained on historically flawed human data risk institutionalizing and amplifying societal discrimination.
