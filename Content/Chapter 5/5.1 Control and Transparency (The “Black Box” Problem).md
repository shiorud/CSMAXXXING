### 5.1 Control and Transparency (The “Black Box” Problem)
Traditional programming offers developers full control over the logic and behavior of software, allowing for precise
customization and predictable, consistent outcomes. And if a program doesn’t behave as expected, developers can
trace back through the codebase to identify and correct the issue. AI systems, particularly complex models like deep
neural networks, can be hard to control and interpret. They often work like “black boxes,” where the input and output
are known, but the process the model uses to get from one to the other is unclear. This lack of transparency can be
problematic in industries that prioritize process and decision-making explainability (like healthcare and finance).
