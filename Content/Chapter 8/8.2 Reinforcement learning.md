### 8.2 Reinforcement learning

In **reinforcement learning (RL)**, an **agent** learns by interacting with an **environment**. It takes an action, the environment responds with a new situation and a **reward** (or penalty), and the agent adjusts to earn more reward over time. The strategy it learns is called a **policy**. Unlike supervised learning, RL does not rely on labelled examples; it learns by **trial and error** (OVHcloud; Verpex).

Two ideas to remember:
- **Exploration vs exploitation:** try new things, or repeat what already worked?
- **Delayed rewards:** some actions pay off only later, which makes learning harder.
