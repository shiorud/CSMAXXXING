### 6.2 Strengths and limits

| Strength | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Explainable** | You can trace a conclusion back to the exact rules used |
| **Easy to edit** | Change a rule and the behaviour changes |
| **Needs little data** | The knowledge is written by people, not learned from examples |

| Limit | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Needs well-defined knowledge** | If the rules are incomplete, the system can fail |
| **Hard to scale** | More symbols and rules can make reasoning very expensive |
| **Poor with uncertainty** | It prefers precise, unambiguous facts |
| **Limited learning** | It usually must be reprogrammed by hand |

**Common misconception:** "Symbolic AI is old, so it's useless." Not true. Rule-based reasoning is still used where explanations matter, and symbolic and learning-based methods can be **combined** into hybrid systems (DataCamp, 2023).
