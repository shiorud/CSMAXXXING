### 7.1 What is connectionism?

**Connectionism** explains intelligent behaviour using **artificial neural networks** (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy). A network is made of many simple **units** (loosely inspired by brain cells) joined by connections. Each connection has a **weight** that says how strongly one unit influences another. Weights can be positive or negative.

Units are usually grouped into three kinds:
- **Input units** receive the information to be processed.
- **Hidden units** sit in between and pass signals along.
- **Output units** hold the result.

Each unit works out its own activation from the signals it receives, then passes it on. **The knowledge of the network lives in its weights.**
