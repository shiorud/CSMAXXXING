### 3.2 Artificial General AI (Strong AI) – Theoretical Concepts

Core Concept: General AI is a theoretical machine intelligence matching human cognitive flexibility. It possesses the ability to reason, plan, solve abstract problems, and transfer knowledge across unrelated domains.

Operational Goals: A Strong AI system would understand context, learn from minimal sensory input, display self-awareness, and independently formulate creative ideas without explicit reprogramming.

The Development Frontier: No true General AI exists today. Modern Large Language Models mimic cross-domain competencies but still rely on underlying statistical correlation rather than authentic cognitive understanding.

Engineering Hurdles: Creating conscious logic requires solving the grounding problem—how symbols or words gain true real-world meaning—and modeling complex human-like executive function.
