### 3.3 Super AI – Beyond Human Cognition
Core Concept: Super AI represents a hypothetical evolutionary state where machine intelligence far surpasses collective human capability across all fields. This includes scientific creativity, general wisdom, social skills, and strategic planning.

The Singularity Event: This tier assumes a milestone where an autonomous General AI enters an exponential recursive self-improvement loop, rapidly rebuilding its own code base faster than human developers can track.

Cognitive Scale: A Super AI would process multi-disciplinary variables simultaneously, uncovering physical laws or solving systemic global issues completely incomprehensible to human minds.

Safety & Alignment Risk: Because its cognitive processing scales beyond human oversight, ensuring its operational goals align with human survival remains a paramount theoretical challenge in AI safety.
