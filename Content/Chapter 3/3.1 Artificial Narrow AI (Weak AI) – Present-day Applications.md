### 3.1 Artificial Narrow AI (Weak AI) – Present-day Applications

Core Concept: Narrow AI describes systems engineered and trained for one specific operational task. They operate under constraints and cannot autonomously apply their intelligence to unlearned domains.

Algorithmic Limits: These systems excel at processing parameters within their defined boundaries but lack actual understanding, context-switching capabilities, or common sense.

Real-World Execution: Industrial image processing, autonomous vehicles, internet search engines, spam filters, and financial fraud detection systems represent current applications.

The "Weak" Paradox: While labeled "weak," these systems routinely outperform human experts in speed, mathematical precision, and raw data capacity within their designated sandbox.
