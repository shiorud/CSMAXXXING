### 7.2 How does it learn?

Finding good weights is the central goal. Learning methods such as **backpropagation** calculate adjustments to the weights so the network does better on its training examples. Training often takes a huge number of rounds of adjustment (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy).

**Misconception check:** artificial neural networks are *inspired by* the brain but are **much simpler** than real brains. "Neuron" here means a small calculation, not a living cell.
