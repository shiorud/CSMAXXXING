### 7.3 Symbolic vs connectionist

| | Symbolic | Connectionist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge comes from | Humans writing rules | Patterns learned from data |
| Explains its answers? | Usually yes | Often hard (the "black box", Section 5.1) |
| Needs lots of data? | No | Usually yes |
| Good at | Logic, structured knowledge | Perception, such as images |
